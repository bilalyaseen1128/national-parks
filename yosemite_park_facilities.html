Yosemite National Park - Facilities

Top 5 Facilities Available for Visitors

Visitor / Welcome Centres

The park features visitor centres where you can pick up maps, get advice from rangers, view exhibits about geology, history and wildlife. These are excellent first-stops to orient yourself for your visit. National Park Service Arrive early to avoid crowds, and ask about any trail closures or shuttle info.

Lodging

Yosemite offers in-park lodging from upscale historic hotels (e.g., the iconic The Ahwahnee) to lodges and cabins. National Park Service These accommodations often fill up well in advance — book early if staying overnight inside the park.

Campgrounds

There are many campgrounds throughout the park accommodating tents and RVs, with varying degrees of amenities. Reservations are strongly recommended. National Park Service. Choose a location depending on which part of the park you plan to explore (Valley vs High Country) and check facility details (e.g., water, bathrooms).

Dining Areas

Within Yosemite Valley and other populated areas of the park you’ll find restaurants, cafés, stores, and other visitor services (retail, food & beverage, rentals). National Park Service. For remote areas check opening seasons and hours — in less busy seasons some outlets may be limited.
